# Privacy Policy

## ShellAlpha

ShellAlpha is an open-source, privacy-first Lite Apps browser and web container for Android.

## What information do you collect?
We do not collect, transmit, sell, or analyze any personal data or usage telemetry. All site data, settings, cookies, and credentials remain entirely local on your device.

When you enable sandboxes, each sandbox runs in an isolated process with its own dedicated data directory. When you enable "Clear cookies after usage", your session cookies are purged upon app termination.
